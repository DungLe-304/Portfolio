# Reference Brief: Vercel's precision × Anthropic's warmth

Date: 2026-11-24. Studied at 1440px and 375px with Playwright. This brief records principles only. No assets, color values, copy, or layouts are taken from either site.

## vercel.com: structure and interaction
- **Visible grid.** Content sits in cells drawn with 1px hairlines, so the page feels engineered and measured. The lines do the organizing; few boxes need fills or shadows.
- **Varied rhythm.** Big statement rows alternate with dense rows of product cells. A wide cell next to a narrow column of feature links breaks the even scroll.
- **Two type roles.** A tightly tracked geometric sans (around -0.06em on headlines) carries the voice, while monospace marks anything technical: labels, metadata, numbers. One family and two widths keep it calm.
- **Low-contrast chrome, high-contrast content.** Off-white near neutral, near-black text, and gray for secondary words in the same sentence ("100 million *monthly*"). Accent color is almost absent, so emphasis comes from weight and value.
- **Small radii (about 6px), pill buttons, one solid primary.** Each action row has one filled button and one outlined button.
- **Motion is local.** Illustrations fade into the grid and cells respond on hover. The page itself never moves dramatically.

## anthropic.com: color and tone
- **Warm paper, not white.** A slightly yellowed off-white makes the page read like print and lowers glare. Dark sections are warm near-black rather than blue-black.
- **A serif that speaks.** Headlines mix a bold sans lead-in with a lighter serif continuation, so one sentence reads as both statement and voice. Body copy is a humanist sans at a generous size (about 21px) and line height.
- **Generous space and simple rules.** Section labels sit between thin full-width rules. Cards use soft fills and large radii instead of borders.
- **Illustration as texture.** Line drawings, grids, and collage give each topic its own character without stock imagery.
- **Restraint.** Pill-shaped quiet controls, very little motion, no gradients on UI chrome.

## What we take for this portfolio
| Principle | From | How it shows up here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A 1px grid that organizes content | Vercel | Bento cells for highlights, projects, skills, and the closing band |
| Mono for anything measured | Vercel | Stats, dates, stack tags, and labels in Geist Mono with tabular numerals |
| One solid primary action | Vercel | "Resume (PDF)" is the filled button; GitHub, LinkedIn, and Email are outlined |
| Warm paper with warm dark | Anthropic | A cream default and a charcoal-brown dark mode, with our own values |
| A serif only for voice | Anthropic | Hero statement and section headlines; everything else in Geist |
| Original line illustrations | Anthropic | Unit-count dot grids and simple line glyphs we draw ourselves |
| Calm motion | Both | 150–300ms hovers, fades, and count-ups, all off under reduced motion |

## What we avoid
- Logos, illustrations, proprietary fonts (both sites use custom faces), exact colors, copy, or section layouts.
- Dark-by-default, giant display names, and the uniform rule + heading + block rhythm of the current site.
- Decorative charts that look like data we don't have.
